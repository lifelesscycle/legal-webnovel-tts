Webnovel TTS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026
Personal application. Webnovel TTS is a privately self-deployed application created solely for personal, non-commercial use. It is not operated as a business, organization, or public service.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Webnovel TTS handles information when it is used by its owner.

Webnovel TTS is not a public service and is not intended to be used by members of the public. It is maintained solely for personal use.

2. Information Processed by Webnovel TTS

Webnovel TTS may process information necessary for its personal functionality, including:

  • Text or other content selected for processing.
  • Files selected or created through the application.
  • Text-to-speech output generated by the application.
  • Application settings and configuration data.
  • Technical information necessary for the application to operate.

Webnovel TTS does not intentionally collect information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or commercial purposes.

3. Google Drive Access

Webnovel TTS uses Google Drive as storage for files used and generated by the application.

The application uses Google's OAuth authorization system to obtain permission to access the authorized Google Drive account.

Webnovel TTS requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file Google Drive scope.

This permission is used to allow Webnovel TTS to work with files that are made available to the application through the Google Drive API.

Depending on the application's operation, Webnovel TTS may use this access to:

  • Read files available to the application.
  • Download files from Google Drive.
  • Upload files generated by the application.
  • Create files in Google Drive.
  • Retrieve files previously created or made available to the application.

Webnovel TTS does not intentionally request or use full unrestricted access to the user's Google Drive.

4. Google OAuth

Google OAuth is used to authorize Webnovel TTS to communicate with Google Drive.

Webnovel TTS does not receive or store the Google account password used during authorization.

Authentication and authorization are handled through Google's OAuth infrastructure.

The authorization credentials provided to the application are used only to perform the Google Drive operations required by Webnovel TTS.

5. How Google Drive Data Is Used

Google Drive data accessed by Webnovel TTS is used only to provide the application's personal file-processing and storage functionality.

For example, the application may download a text file, process its contents, generate text-to-speech audio, and upload the resulting audio file to Google Drive.

Google Drive data is not intentionally:

  • Sold or rented.
  • Used for advertising.
  • Used to create advertising profiles.
  • Shared with other users through Webnovel TTS.
  • Used for unrelated commercial purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Webnovel TTS may use third-party services required for its functionality, including Google Drive and Google OAuth.

Third-party services operate independently of Webnovel TTS and may process information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and technical systems.

7. Temporary Data

Files downloaded from Google Drive may temporarily exist in the application's runtime environment while they are being processed.

Generated files may also temporarily exist locally before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Temporary files may be deleted after processing depending on the application's implementation and configuration.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Webnovel TTS does not operate a separate permanent cloud-storage service.

Files stored through the application are stored in the authorized Google Drive account.

The application's owner controls those files through Google Drive and may delete them at any time.

Google Drive files may remain in the account until they are manually deleted or otherwise removed according to Google's applicable systems.

9. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect the application's configuration, credentials, and stored information.

However, no computer system, network, storage service, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security of the Google account and credentials used with Webnovel TTS is the responsibility of the account owner.

10. Children's Privacy

Webnovel TTS is a private personal application and is not operated as a service for children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality, Google API usage, or data handling changes.

The latest version will replace previous versions when published.

12. Contact
